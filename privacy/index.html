Privacy

When you send the demo form we receive your name, work email, company, website (if you give it) and what you are interested in. We also keep where the click came from (the campaign tag in the link) so we know which message reached you.

What we do with it

We use it only to reply to you, book the call and look at your website before we talk. We do not sell it or share it with anyone else.

How long we keep it

Up to 24 months after our last conversation, then we delete it. You can ask us to delete it sooner at any time.
